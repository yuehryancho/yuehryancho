### Yueh (Ryan) Cho

Assistant Professor, Bachelor Program of Biotechnology and Food and Nutrition & Institute of Biotechnology, National Taiwan University

My lab studies how plant cells keep protein homeostasis running under stress: how ribosome collisions and translational pauses are resolved, how the ER senses and clears folding overload, and how membrane lipids shape organelle proteostasis. We work with *Arabidopsis* and the red alga *Cyanidioschyzon merolae*, using Ribo-seq, Disome-seq, phosphoproteomics, lipidomics, and CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing, with the aim of engineering stress-resilient crops.

The lab is recruiting a postdoctoral researcher, PhD and MS students, and undergraduate researchers.
